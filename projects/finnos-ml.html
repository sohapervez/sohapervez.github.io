The Problem

Sawmill operations at Finnos Oy relied on manual quality judgement and rule-based processes for sawing decisions. These were time-consuming, inconsistent across operators, and left measurable accuracy gains on the table. The goal was to replace subjective decisions with data-driven ML models.

What I Built

Four machine learning models designed for different aspects of sawing operations, trained on production data and integrated into Finnos Oy's live workflow. Each model was validated against real operational outcomes before deployment.

The full technical stack and model architectures are confidential (this was a work project), but the overall approach covered feature engineering from sensor and operational data, model selection and tuning, and deployment into the production environment.

⚠ Code is private — work project at Finnos Oy

The Result

35% improvement in sawing accuracy.

All four models were integrated into live production operations at Finnos Oy, replacing manual decisions with automated, consistent predictions. The 35% accuracy improvement translated directly into reduced material waste and higher operational efficiency across the sawmill's production line.
